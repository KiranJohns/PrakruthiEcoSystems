Our Journey

From One Workshop to A Statewide Presence

2015
Started Manufacturing Aluva Smokeless Ovens
Started with a focus on efficient, cleaner cooking solutions designed for homes and businesses.
↓
2017
Expanded Installation Services
Extended our reach by providing professional installation and on-site support for customers across Kerala.
↓
2019
Expanded into Sustainable Waste Solutions
Added practical waste-management solutions to help homes and institutions handle waste more responsibly.
↓
2021
Introduced Stainless Steel Waste Incinerators
Expanded our product range with durable incinerators designed for residential, commercial and institutional applications.
↓
2023
Introduced Biogas Plant Solutions
Added biogas systems that convert kitchen and organic waste into useful cooking gas and nutrient-rich organic slurry.
↓
2024
Established Dedicated Repair & Maintenance Services
Strengthened our after-sales support with installation assistance, repairs, maintenance and Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC).
↓
2026
Expanding Across Kerala
Today, Prakruthi Eco Systems provides eco-friendly cooking, waste-management and biogas solutions to homes, hospitals, schools, resorts and businesses across Kerala.
