FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our products and services.

Which waste can be burned in the incinerator? ▼
Our waste incinerators are designed to safely process food waste, paper, garden waste, dry waste, and organic waste. They are suitable for homes, institutions, hospitals, resorts, and commercial buildings.
Can the biogas plant reduce LPG usage? ▼
Yes, a properly installed biogas plant can significantly reduce your LPG consumption by providing clean cooking gas from kitchen waste. Many customers report up to 40-60% reduction in LPG usage.
Do you provide repair services? ▼
Absolutely. We offer comprehensive repair and maintenance services for all our products — Waste Incinerators, Biogas Plants, and Smokeless Ovens. Our trained technicians are available across Kerala.
How long is the warranty? ▼
We offer a 2-Year Warranty on all Aluva Smokeless Ovens and up to 5 years on our Waste Incinerators and Biogas Plants, depending on the model and installation type.
How long does installation take? ▼
Installation typically takes 2-5 days depending on the product and site conditions. We provide a detailed timeline during the consultation phase.
