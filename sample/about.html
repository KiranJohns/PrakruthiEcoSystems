Our Journey

A Decade of Purposeful Innovation

From a single workshop to Kerala's most trusted clean-tech partner — every step forward has been guided by a commitment to sustainability and quality.

2015
Started with Aluva Smokeless Ovens
Our journey began with a vision to make traditional cooking healthier and more efficient. The first smokeless oven was crafted with precision, reducing smoke and fuel consumption dramatically.
↓
2017
Expanded Fabrication Capabilities
We invested in advanced stainless steel fabrication equipment, enabling us to scale production while maintaining the highest quality standards.
↓
2019
Introduced Waste Incinerators
Recognizing the growing need for responsible waste disposal, we engineered premium incinerators for residential, institutional and commercial use.
↓
2021
Started Carbon Neutral Biogas Solutions
We launched our biogas plant range — converting kitchen waste into clean cooking gas and organic fertilizer, helping families reduce their carbon footprint.
↓
2023
Added Professional Repair & Maintenance Services
To ensure long-term reliability, we established a dedicated service division offering installation, repair, and Annual Maintenance Contracts across Kerala.
↓
2026
Serving Customers Across Kerala
Today, we proudly serve homes, schools, hospitals, resorts, and businesses in all 14 districts — a testament to the trust and relationships we've built.
