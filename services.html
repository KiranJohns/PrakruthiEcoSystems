FAQ

Practical Questions

What type of waste can be processed?▼
Organic kitchen waste, dry waste, paper, garden clippings, and non-recyclable sanitary items can be processed through our systems.
Which solution is suitable for my property?▼
The right solution depends on your waste volume, space availability, and energy needs. Our team assesses these factors during the site visit.
Do you provide installation across Kerala?▼
Yes, we offer installation services across all 14 districts of Kerala.
Are maintenance services available?▼
We provide comprehensive maintenance, repair, and Annual Maintenance Contracts for all our installations.
Can existing systems be repaired?▼
Yes, we offer repair and refurbishment services for existing systems regardless of the original installer.
How long does installation usually take?▼
Standard installations are completed within 48 to 72 hours post-inspection. Custom projects may take 7–10 business days.
